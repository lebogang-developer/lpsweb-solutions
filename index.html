Our Story

From Bloemfontein, For South Africa

LPS Web Solutions was founded in 2023 with a simple but powerful conviction: South African small businesses deserve world-class digital solutions at prices that make sense.

We watched great local businesses — law firms, medical practices, restaurants, contractors — lose customers to competitors simply because their websites were outdated or nonexistent. We decided to do something about it.

Today, LPS Web Solutions has helped over 10 businesses across the Free State and beyond establish powerful online presences. We remain proudly local, personally invested in every client, and relentlessly focused on delivering results that matter.
